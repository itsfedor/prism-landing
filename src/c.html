How it works

Extract first.
Generate second.

All six pipelines run the same three steps — and none of them writes a word before the source is in hand.

1

Extract

The pipeline pulls the raw material first: a transcript, a voice note, a script, the text of an exercise. Nothing gets written until the source is in hand.

transcript · script · audio → structured source
2

Generate

A fixed structure plus level-aware prompts build the artifact in your house style: vocabulary taken from the source, the same exercise layout every time. Quoted lines get checked against the source before anything ships.

source + level contract → your house style
3

Deliver

It ships as a file you can paste, a summary you can send, or a lesson written straight into your own Edvibe account through a local CLI.

artifact → Edvibe · Notion · your student

Nothing in the middle. Everything runs on your own machine, under your own Edvibe login. The password is typed once, in your terminal, and never leaves it; the session stays cached on your computer, and nothing is shared between teachers. Quoted lines are checked against the source programmatically — nothing reaches a student before you have read it.
